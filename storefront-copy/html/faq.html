Shopping the collection

Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?
Add sarees to your bag, continue to delivery, and prepare a WhatsApp message. Review the message and press Send in WhatsApp. The store must confirm availability, shipping charges, and the order before it is accepted.
What happens when I use the WELCOME10 coupon?
The demo subtracts 10% of your bag subtotal and shows the updated estimate. Mention the coupon in your WhatsApp message and ask the store to confirm the offer applies to your final order.
Can I confirm the fabric, colour, and blouse piece?
Product details are shown on each product page. Colours can look different across screens, so contact the store to confirm fabric, measurements, blouse-piece inclusion, care instructions, and current availability before ordering.
How much is shipping and when will it arrive?
The demo banner shows free shipping on orders of ₹999 and above. Delivery area, carrier, timeline, and any final shipping charge must be confirmed with the store; this demo does not calculate live shipping.
Can I pay online or track an order here?
No. This frontend demo does not process payments, create production orders, or provide live tracking. Do not treat the WhatsApp draft as an accepted order. Confirm payment and delivery details with the store.
What is the return or exchange policy?
Return and exchange eligibility depends on the store's policy and the item. Please confirm the current policy with the store before paying or confirming an order. See Shipping & Returns.
Where are my bag and wishlist saved?
They are saved in local storage in this browser on this device. They are not attached to an account and may disappear if browser data is cleared.
