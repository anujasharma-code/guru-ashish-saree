Privacy & terms

A transparent browser demo

Learn what this storefront saves, what happens when you prepare a WhatsApp message, and which information still needs confirmation from the shop.

Privacy in this demo

Your shopping bag, wishlist, theme preference, coupon, and newsletter demo email are stored in local storage in the browser you use. This site does not have a server or customer account database. Clearing browser data may remove these saved preferences.

Enquiry and delivery details are used in a WhatsApp message draft in your browser; this storefront does not save them. If you choose to send the draft, its contents are shared with the WhatsApp recipient you select.

Fonts, icons, and product photography may load from third-party services such as Google Fonts, Font Awesome, and Unsplash.

Storefront terms

Catalogue descriptions, prices, stock, and promotional details are sample storefront data. The store must confirm product specifications, availability, shipping, return eligibility, and the final amount before accepting an order.

No payment is processed, no secure account is provided, and no production order or delivery tracking is created by this website. A prepared WhatsApp message is only an enquiry until you send it and the store confirms the request.

For actual purchase terms, contact the store before making payment.
