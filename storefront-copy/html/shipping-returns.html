Clear expectations

What this storefront can and cannot confirm

This is a front-end demo. It has no live courier rates, delivery tracking, or order-management system. The store must confirm the final terms with you directly.

Shipping & delivery

The demo banner shows free shipping on orders of ₹999 and above. For an actual order, ask the store to confirm whether the offer applies, the serviceable PIN code, delivery estimate, and any shipping charges.

Shipping charges are excluded from the estimated total when they have not been confirmed.

Returns & exchanges

Eligibility can depend on the saree and the store's current policy. Contact the store and confirm the return window, condition requirements, and process before confirming payment.

This demo does not promise or process a return, refund, or exchange.

Product checks

Ask the store to confirm colour, fabric, measurements, blouse-piece inclusion, care instructions, and stock for your selected item. Product photos and availability in the catalogue are demo information.

Payment & confirmation

No payment is collected on this website. A WhatsApp draft is not an accepted order. Confirm payment method, final price, shipping, and delivery directly with the store before paying.
